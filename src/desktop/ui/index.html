What should Conductor do?

Conductor plans the work, routes each part to Claude, Gemini or Codex by the rules in routing.yaml, and checks what comes back before answering. Each delegation shows up as a thread in the sidebar.

  • @gemini, @claude or @codex pins a backend for this request.
  • /route code.review gemini pins one kind of task.
  • @ mentions a file, / lists commands, and a pasted image is attached.
  • Ctrl K searches every chat; earlier chats are in the sidebar.
